MobilePlay privacy information
Developer: Sarthak Khedkar

Pairing and playback
MobilePlay uses local network access to discover your console, pair with it,
and exchange gameplay video, audio and controller input. Pairing uses the PSN
account identifier and the pairing code you provide. Saved pairing credentials
are encrypted on your device using Android Keystore.

Sony sign-in
When you choose Sony browser sign-in, the app opens Sony's site in your browser.
Sony handles the sign-in. Only paste the requested Sony redirect into MobilePlay.
MobilePlay never asks you to type your Sony password into the app.

Microphone
The microphone starts muted. If you enable it during a stream and grant
Android microphone permission, MobilePlay captures your voice and sends it to
the connected console. MobilePlay stops capture when you mute, disconnect, or
leave the foreground. You can revoke permission in Android app settings.

Settings and deletion
Control profiles, console settings and preferences are stored on this device.
Remove a saved console to delete its pairing credentials from MobilePlay.
Clear MobilePlay's app storage in Android settings to remove other local data.
